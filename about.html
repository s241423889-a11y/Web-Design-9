About Me

My name is Robin Lim, aged 53, born in Kedah. I studied Hotel Management and previously worked as an assisting contractor. Later, I decided to start my own business in agriculture. I am now the owner of Super Fruits Valley, a farm located in Chuping, Perlis.

I have been running this business for over 10 years and I am passionate about providing high-quality fruits to my customers. I also cultivate a variety of crops including vegetables and local fruits. My goal is to deliver fresh, high-quality produce to the community while maintaining sustainable farming practices.

Background:

  • Operating for 10 years (2016 – present)
  • Focused on fresh fruit cultivation
  • Consistent and sustainable business operations

Skills & Expertise:

  • Farm and crop management
  • Fertigation systems and modern farming techniques
  • Crop care and quality control
  • Distribution of agricultural produce

Roles & Responsibilities:

  • Managing daily farm operations
  • Ensuring high-quality produce
  • Supplying fresh fruits to customers
